## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient |
|--------|------------|
| 800 g  | medium waxy potatoes |
| 4 tbsp | olive oil |
| 2      | peppers |
| 2      | onions (medium) |
| 60 g   | parmesan, grated |
|        | thyme |
|        | salt |

## Preparation

1. Wash the potatoes, cut out any blemishes and slice them like a fan without cutting through.
2. Place on a tray lined with baking paper, brush with oil and salt.
3. Wash the peppers and cut into thin strips. Peel the onions and slice into thin rings. Put them next to the potatoes and drizzle with a little oil too.
4. Bake in the preheated oven at 170 °C fan for about 45 minutes until golden.
5. After 30 minutes brush once more with the oil that has run off and stir the vegetables.
6. After 40 minutes sprinkle the thyme and parmesan over the potatoes.
