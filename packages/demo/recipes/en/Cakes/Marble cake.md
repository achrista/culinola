## Ingredients

| Amount        | Ingredient |
|---------------|------------|
| 300 g         | margarine or butter |
| 275 g         | sugar |
| 1 sachet      | vanilla sugar |
|               | salt |
| 5             | eggs |
| 375 g         | plain flour |
| 4 level tsp   | baking powder |
| about 3 tbsp  | milk |

### Dark batter

| Amount   | Ingredient |
|----------|------------|
| 20 g     | cocoa powder |
| 20 g     | sugar |
| 2–3 tbsp | milk |

### For dusting

| Amount | Ingredient |
|--------|------------|
|        | icing sugar |

## Preparation

1. Beat the fat with a hand mixer until smooth, gradually beating in the sugar and vanilla sugar until well combined.
2. Beat in the eggs one at a time (about ½ minute each).
3. Mix the baking powder with the flour, sift, and stir in on medium speed a spoonful at a time, alternating with the milk (only enough milk for the batter to drop heavily from a spoon).
4. Pour two thirds of the batter into a loaf or bundt tin.
5. Stir the cocoa, sugar and milk into the rest of the batter so that it again drops heavily from the spoon.
6. Spread the dark batter over the light one and draw a fork through the layers in a spiral to make the marble pattern.
7. Bake: top and bottom heat 170–200 °C (preheated) or fan 160–170 °C (not preheated), 50–65 minutes.
8. Dust the cooled cake with icing sugar.
