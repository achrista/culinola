## Ingredients

### Quark and oil dough

| Amount       | Ingredient |
|--------------|------------|
| 150 g        | quark |
| 6 tbsp       | oil |
| 1            | egg |
| 2 tbsp       | milk (as needed) |
| 75 g         | sugar |
| 1 pinch      | salt |
|              | lemon zest or vanilla sugar |
| 300 g        | plain flour |
| ¾–1 sachet   | baking powder |

### Topping

| Amount     | Ingredient |
|------------|------------|
| 1–1 ½ kg   | plums (or apples) |
| some       | butter |
| some       | sugar (or cinnamon sugar) |

## Preparation

1. Mix all the dough ingredients except the flour well. Stir in half of the flour mixed and sifted with the baking powder.
2. Quickly knead in the rest of the flour.
3. Roll out, brush with melted butter and cover with the fruit.
4. Bake at about 200 °C for 25–30 minutes. Sprinkle with a little sugar 5 minutes before the end.

## Tips

- Best eaten fresh, but freezes well.
- For apple cake: cover with foil for the first half of the baking time. Let it cool briefly, take it off the tray, then sprinkle with sugar.
