## Ingredients

### Dough

| Amount          | Ingredient |
|-----------------|------------|
| 125 g           | margarine or butter |
| 125 g           | sugar |
| 1 sachet        | vanilla sugar |
|                 | salt |
| ½ small bottle  | lemon baking oil |
| 3               | eggs |
| 200 g           | plain flour |
| 2 level tsp     | baking powder |
| 1–2 tbsp        | milk |

### Topping

| Amount | Ingredient |
|--------|------------|
| 750 g  | apples |
| 25 g   | melted butter |
| 40 g   | raisins |

### Glaze

| Amount | Ingredient |
|--------|------------|
| 2 tbsp | sieved apricot jam |
| 1 tbsp | water |

## Preparation

1. Beat the margarine or butter with a hand mixer on the highest setting until smooth. Gradually beat in the sugar, vanilla sugar, salt and baking oil until well combined.
2. Beat in the eggs one at a time (about ½ minute each).
3. Mix the flour with the baking powder, sift, and stir in on medium speed a spoonful at a time, alternating with the milk (use only enough milk for the batter to drop heavily from a spoon).
4. Pour the batter into a springform tin (Ø 28 cm, base greased) and smooth the top.
5. For the topping, peel, quarter and core the apples, score them lengthwise several times and arrange them in a ring on the batter. Brush with the melted butter and scatter with raisins if you like.
6. Bake: top and bottom heat 170–200 °C (preheated), fan 160–170 °C (not preheated), gas mark 3–4 (not preheated), 40–50 minutes.
7. For the glaze, bring the jam and water to the boil while stirring and brush the cake with it straight after baking.

## Tips

- Variation: use 600 g pitted sour cherries instead of the apples.
