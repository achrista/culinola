## Ingredients

| Amount          | Ingredient |
|-----------------|------------|
| 1 medium        | onion |
| 1 clove         | garlic |
| 10 g            | parsley |
| 10 g            | marjoram |
| 50 g            | walnuts |
| 2 tins of 285 g | kidney beans |
| 2 tbsp          | mustard |
| 65 g            | rolled oats |
| 3 tbsp          | breadcrumbs |
| 2               | eggs |
| 2 tbsp          | olive oil |
| 80 ml           | milk |
| 3 tbsp          | clarified butter |
|                 | salt, pepper |

## Preparation

1. Soak the oats in a little milk for at least half an hour.
2. Peel and finely chop the onion and garlic; chop the parsley finely (or use dried). Chop the walnuts finely. Soften the onion, garlic and parsley in a little fat, then add the walnuts.
3. Drain the kidney beans in a sieve, rinse and let them drip. Mash them well with a spoon in a bowl, a portion at a time.
4. Add all the other ingredients and mix. Season well with salt and pepper and shape into patties by hand.
5. Heat the clarified butter in a pan and fry the patties on both sides over medium heat until golden brown.
