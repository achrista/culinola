## Ingredients

### Basic recipe

| Amount         | Ingredient |
|----------------|------------|
| 250 g          | flour |
| 3              | eggs (medium) |
| ½ cup          | water |
| 1 level tsp    | salt |
| 1 level tsp    | turmeric (just for colour) |
| 1 tbsp         | sunflower oil |

### For cheese spätzle, also

| Amount | Ingredient |
|--------|------------|
| 250 g  | mountain cheese, grated (or another strong cheese) |
| 2–4    | onions, medium (to taste) |
| 1 tbsp | sunflower oil |
|        | pepper |

## Preparation

### Spätzle

1. Beat the flour, water, eggs, salt and turmeric in a bowl with a mixer until there are no lumps. The batter should be thick. Leave it to rest for at least 10 minutes.
2. Bring plenty of salted water with a spoonful of oil to the boil in a large pot. The pot should be at least 5 cm higher than the water.
3. Scrape the spätzle into the gently boiling water with a spätzle board (or a potato ricer) and loosen them with a slotted spoon. Cook for 2 minutes, lift out with the slotted spoon and drain well. Repeat depending on the size of the pot.
4. Rinse bowl and tools in cold or lukewarm water.

### Cheese spätzle

1. Grease a baking dish with a little oil. Layer the spätzle and grated cheese in it. Pour some (50 ml) of the cooking water over and spread the rest of the cheese on top.
2. Bake at 180 °C for about 20 minutes until the cheese is browned.
3. Peel the onions and slice into thin rings. Fry in a tablespoon of sunflower oil until golden brown.
4. Season the baked spätzle with freshly ground pepper and top with the onions.
