## Ingredients

### Pizza dough

| Amount     | Ingredient |
|------------|------------|
| 350 g      | plain flour |
| ½ sachet   | yeast |
| 2 tsp      | salt |
| ½ tsp      | sugar |
| 170 ml     | lukewarm water |
| 1          | egg yolk |
| 15 g       | olive oil |

### Tomato sauce

| Amount | Ingredient |
|--------|------------|
| 1 tbsp | olive oil |
| ½      | dried chopped chilli |
| 1 tsp  | chopped parsley |
| 1 tsp  | oregano |
| 1 tsp  | chopped garlic |
| ½ tin  | tomatoes (480 g drained weight) |
|        | salt, sugar, freshly ground pepper |

### Cheese

| Amount | Ingredient |
|--------|------------|
| 250 g  | mozzarella, grated |

## Preparation

### Pizza dough

1. Put the flour, yeast, salt and sugar in a bowl and mix well.
2. Whisk the egg yolk and olive oil into the water, add and knead thoroughly.
3. Leave to rise for at least 4 hours; for longer, put it somewhere cool.
4. Roll out the dough, top it and place it on baking paper. Then slide it onto the hot tray in the preheated oven.
5. Bake: top and bottom heat 250 °C for about 12–15 min, or on the "pizza" setting at 275 °C for about 10 min.

### Tomato sauce

1. Heat the olive oil in a large pan. Gently fry the chilli, parsley, oregano and garlic. Don't let the garlic brown or it turns bitter.
2. Add the tomatoes. Season with freshly ground pepper, salt and a little sugar.
3. Briefly blend with a stick blender. Let the sauce reduce and cool.
