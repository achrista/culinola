## Ingredients

| Amount           | Ingredient |
|------------------|------------|
| 350 g            | flour |
| 170 ml           | water |
| 24 g (4 tbsp)    | sunflower oil |
| 200 g (1 tub)    | soured cream |
| 40 ml            | cream |
| 150 g            | red onions |
| 125 g            | bacon, diced |
|                  | salt, pepper |

## Preparation

1. Knead the flour, oil, water and salt into a dough. It must not feel sticky – stick exactly to the amounts and it will be fine.
2. Stir the soured cream into the cream.
3. Slice the onions thinly. Roll the dough out very thinly and spread with the cream. Scatter the onions and bacon on top.
4. Slide the tarte on its baking paper onto the hot tray in the preheated oven. Bake at 290 °C for about 10 minutes until the base and edge are crisp.
