## Ingredients

| Amount             | Ingredient |
|--------------------|------------|
| 2                  | medium onions |
| 2 tbsp             | olive oil |
| 1 tsp              | butter |
|                    | salt |
| 4                  | garlic cloves |
| 200 g (½ tin)      | chopped tomatoes |
| ½ tsp              | chilli flakes (or 1 chilli) |
|                    | pepper |
| 200 g              | pasta |
| 150 g              | cream |
| 2 tbsp             | grated parmesan |
| 3 (½ bunch)        | spring onions |
| 1 squeeze          | lemon juice |
| some               | chilli oil & chives to garnish |

## Preparation

1. Onions: halve the onions and cut into thin half rings. Heat the olive oil and butter in a pan, add the onions with salt and cook over medium heat for 20 minutes until golden. Stir now and then and add a splash of water if needed.
2. Add the tomatoes and cook for about 10 minutes more.
3. Flavour: chop the garlic finely and add it to the onions with the chilli flakes. Slice the spring onions and add two thirds. Fry for 2 minutes and season with pepper.
4. Cook the pasta and keep some of the cooking water.
5. Finish the sauce: take the pan off the heat, stir in the cream and parmesan and let it simmer gently.
6. Add the pasta to the sauce and stir with a little cooking water until creamy.
7. To serve, fold in the rest of the spring onions and the lemon juice, and top with chilli oil and chives.
