## Ingredients

| Amount          | Ingredient |
|-----------------|------------|
| 1–2             | medium onions |
| 2 cloves        | garlic |
| 1               | chilli |
| 1 kg            | ready-made gnocchi |
| 800 g (2 tins)  | chopped tomatoes |
| 120 g           | parmesan, grated |
| 2 tbsp          | Mediterranean herbs, thyme, basil |
| 4 tbsp          | olive oil |
| 1 tbsp          | tomato purée |
| 50 ml           | red wine |
| 2 tbsp          | butter |
|                 | salt & pepper |

## Preparation

1. Finely chop the onions, garlic and chilli and soften in the olive oil. Add the tomato purée.
2. Add the tomatoes and herbs and simmer for about 10 minutes. Finally add the red wine and season with salt and pepper.
3. Pour everything into a baking dish. Preheat the oven to 200 °C fan.
4. Wipe out the pan and brown the gnocchi in the butter.
5. Add the gnocchi to the dish and mix well with the tomato sauce. Sprinkle with the parmesan and bake until crisp and brown.
