## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient |
|--------|------------|
| 500 g  | waxy potatoes |
| 150 g  | onions |
| 3–(4)  | eggs |
| 90 ml  | olive oil |
|        | salt, pepper |

## Preparation

1. Peel the potatoes, cut into small (about 1 cm) cubes and season with salt. Cook in about 45 ml (3 tbsp) olive oil in a non-stick pan over medium heat for about 25 minutes, turning often.
2. Take the potatoes out of the pan with a sieve so that the remaining oil stays in the pan.
3. Peel and finely chop the onions. Fry in a little olive oil for about 10–15 minutes until soft and golden.
4. Beat the eggs in a bowl with salt and a little pepper. Add the potatoes and onions and leave for at least 3 minutes.
5. Heat the remaining oil well, pour in the mixture and fry for about 2 minutes. When it starts to brown (the eggs are not set yet), turn the tortilla with a plate: put the plate on top, flip the pan so the tortilla lies on the plate, then slide it back into the pan.
6. Fry the other side for 1–2 minutes. The tortilla should still be runny inside.
