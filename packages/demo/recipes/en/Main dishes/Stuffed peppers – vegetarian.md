## Ingredients

| Amount | Ingredient |
|--------|------------|
| 180 g  | rice |
| 4      | large peppers |
| 1      | onion |
| 1      | carrot |
| 2      | garlic cloves |
| 200 g  | chopped tomatoes, tinned |
| 1      | chilli |
| 100 g  | feta |
| 100 g  | mozzarella, grated |
| 1 tbsp | tomato purée |
| ½ tsp  | curry powder |
| ½ tsp  | paprika |
| ½ tsp  | thyme |
| 1 tbsp | olive oil |
|        | salt & pepper |

## Preparation

1. First cook the rice with a little salt until soft.
2. Wash the peppers, cut off the tops and remove the seeds.
3. Peel the onion and garlic and dice finely; dice the flesh of the pepper tops too. Fry everything gently in the olive oil.
4. After 5 minutes add the chopped tomatoes, tomato purée and spices and simmer for about 10 minutes. Mix with the rice.
5. Let the filling cool. Cut the feta into small pieces and mix half of the feta and mozzarella into the filling.
6. Stand the peppers in a baking dish (or loaf tin) and fill them. Spread any leftover filling around them and top the peppers with the rest of the cheese.
7. Pour 50 ml water into the dish. Bake in the preheated oven at 180 °C for about 35–45 minutes, until the cheese is browned and the peppers are soft.
