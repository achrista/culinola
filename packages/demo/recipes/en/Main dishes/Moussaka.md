## Ingredients

| Amount         | Ingredient |
|----------------|------------|
| 2              | aubergines |
| 500 g          | potatoes |
| 500 g          | minced meat (or plant-based mince) |
| 80 g           | cheese, grated |
| 570 ml         | milk |
| 40 g           | flour |
| 40 g           | butter (or margarine) |
| 2              | onions, medium |
| 1 clove        | garlic |
| 1              | chilli (to taste) |
| 1 tin (400 g)  | chopped tomatoes |
| 30 g           | tomato purée |
| 2              | eggs |
| 40 g           | breadcrumbs |
|                | spices (cinnamon, cumin, oregano, salt, pepper; white pepper for the white sauce only) |
| 50 ml          | sunflower oil |
| 30 ml          | olive oil |

## Preparation

1. Slice the aubergines (about 1 cm) and fry in the sunflower oil until browned.
2. Boil the potatoes, peel and slice thinly.
3. Meat sauce: dice the onions, garlic and chilli and fry in the olive oil over medium heat. Add the mince and brown it. Add the tomatoes, tomato purée and spices and simmer for at least 20 minutes.
4. Egg sauce: melt the butter in a pan and lower the heat. Gradually stir in the flour and milk. Season with salt and white pepper. Keep stirring over medium heat until the sauce thickens. Add a third of the grated cheese and let it cool.
5. Mix the two egg yolks with two spoonfuls of the sauce, then stir them into the sauce. Beat the egg whites until stiff and carefully fold them in a spoonful at a time.
6. In a greased baking dish, layer potato slices, meat sauce and aubergine slices. Pour the egg sauce over, sprinkle with the rest of the cheese and finally with the breadcrumbs.
7. Bake at 180 °C for 45 to 60 minutes until the breadcrumbs and cheese are browned. Take it out of the oven and wait 5–10 minutes before cutting.
