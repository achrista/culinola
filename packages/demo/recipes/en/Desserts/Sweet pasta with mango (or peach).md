## Ingredients

| Amount          | Ingredient |
|-----------------|------------|
| 1               | ripe mango (or 2 peaches) |
| 400 ml (1 tin)  | coconut milk |
| 100 ml          | cream |
| 1 tbsp          | poppy seeds (optional) |
| 1               | Bourbon vanilla pod (or vanilla extract) |
| 200 g           | penne or other pasta |
| 1 tsp           | salt |
| 1 pinch         | white pepper |

## Preparation

1. Cut the mango flesh into small pieces.
2. Bring the coconut milk and cream to the boil in a large pan, scrape in the seeds of the vanilla pod and add salt.
3. Cook the pasta in it (it takes 3–5 minutes longer than in water). Careful: it boils over quickly.
4. Divide between plates, mix with the fruit and sprinkle with white pepper and poppy seeds if you like.

## Tips

- Garnish with lemon balm or mint leaves.
