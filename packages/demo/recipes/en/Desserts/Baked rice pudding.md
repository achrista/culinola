## Ingredients

| Amount                    | Ingredient |
|---------------------------|------------|
| 125 g (250 g)             | pudding rice |
| 600 g (1200 g)            | milk |
| 1 pinch                   | salt |
| 40 g + 10 g (80 g + 20 g) | butter |
| 40 g + 10 g (80 g + 20 g) | sugar |
| 2 (4)                     | eggs |
| ½ tin (1 tin)             | peaches |

## Preparation

1. Cook the rice in the milk over moderate heat. Careful: it boils over quickly and catches easily.
2. Set aside and leave to cool for about 1 hour.
3. Separate the eggs and beat the whites until stiff, adding some of the sugar (1 tablespoon, 8–10 g, per 2 eggs).
4. Beat the yolks until fluffy, gradually adding the sugar and butter. The butter should be soft but not hot.
5. Stir the cooled rice into the yolk mixture bit by bit. Stir in a third of the beaten whites and gently fold in the rest.
6. Grease a baking dish and fill it. Dot 10–20 g butter in small flakes over the top.
7. Bake at 180 °C fan for about 45 minutes (60 minutes for the large amount).

## Tips

- Goes very well with stewed peaches!
