## Ingredients

| Amount         | Ingredient |
|----------------|------------|
| 250 g          | plain flour |
| 1 pinch        | baking powder |
| 125 g          | sugar |
| 1 sachet       | vanilla sugar |
| 3              | egg yolks |
| 200 g          | margarine or butter |
| 125 g          | ground almonds |

### For dusting

| Amount       | Ingredient |
|--------------|------------|
| about 50 g   | icing sugar |
| 1 sachet     | vanilla sugar |

## Preparation

1. Mix the flour with the baking powder and sift into a mixing bowl. Add the sugar, vanilla sugar, egg yolks, margarine or butter and the ground almonds.
2. Work the ingredients with the dough hooks of a hand mixer, briefly on the lowest setting, then on the highest, then knead into a smooth dough on the worktop. If it is sticky, chill it for a while.
3. Shape the dough into pencil-thick rolls, cut into 5–6 cm pieces, roll the ends a little thinner and place on a tray as crescents.
4. Bake for about 10 minutes: top and bottom heat 170–200 °C (preheated), fan 160–170 °C (not preheated), gas mark 3–4 (preheated).
5. Sift the icing sugar, mix with the vanilla sugar and dust the hot crescents with it.
