## Ingredients

### Dough

| Amount   | Ingredient |
|----------|------------|
| 150 g    | flour |
| ½ tsp    | baking powder |
| 65 g     | sugar |
| 1 sachet | vanilla sugar |
| 1        | egg |
| 65 g     | butter |
| 2 tbsp   | apricot jam |

### Topping

| Amount   | Ingredient |
|----------|------------|
| 100 g    | butter |
| 100 g    | sugar |
| 1 sachet | vanilla sugar |
| 2 tbsp   | water |
| 100 g    | ground hazelnuts |
| 150 g    | roughly chopped hazelnuts |
|          | chocolate glaze |

## Preparation

1. Knead the dough ingredients together and roll out to about 32 × 24 cm.
2. Place on a tray (baking paper) and spread with 2 tbsp apricot jam.
3. For the topping, melt the butter, sugar, vanilla sugar and water and bring to the boil once.
4. Stir 100 g ground and 150 g chopped hazelnuts into the mixture and spread over the dough.
5. Bake at 175 °C for 20–30 minutes.
6. Cut the cooled bake into 8 × 8 cm squares, then halve them diagonally into triangles. Dip the corners in chocolate glaze.

## Tips

- For a full tray, simply double the amounts (they keep for days in the fridge).
- Any other jam works instead of apricot.
- Walnuts work instead of hazelnuts.
- Baking time and temperature are for an electric oven.
